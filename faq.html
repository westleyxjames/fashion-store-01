Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Lunaris Shop

Orders

To place an order on Lunaris Shop:

  1. Browse our products and click "Add to Cart" on items you want
  2. Click the cart icon in the header to review your items
  3. Click "Proceed to Checkout"
  4. Fill in your shipping and payment information
  5. Review your order and click "Place Order"
  6. You'll receive an order confirmation email immediately

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of placement. Please contact us immediately at help@lunarisshop.online with your order number. After this window, orders are processed for shipping and cannot be changed.

You can track your order by:

  • Visiting our Track Order page and entering your order number
  • Clicking the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email
  • Contacting our support team at help@lunarisshop.online

Shipping

Standard Shipping (Free on orders over $50):

  • Delivery: 3-7 business days
  • Cost: $4.99 for orders under $50

Express Shipping:

  • Delivery: 2-3 business days
  • Cost: $14.99

All orders are shipped from our facilities across the United States.

Currently, we only ship within the United States and its territories. We're working on expanding our shipping options and hope to offer international shipping soon!

We take full responsibility for lost or damaged packages. If your order arrives damaged or doesn't arrive at all, please contact us at help@lunarisshop.online within 48 hours of expected delivery. We'll send a replacement or issue a full refund immediately.

Returns & Exchanges

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all products. Items must be:

  • Returned within 30 days of delivery
  • In original condition with tags attached
  • Unworn and unwashed
  • In original packaging

For full details, see our Return Policy page.

To return an item:

  1. Email return@lunarisshop.online with your order number
  2. We'll send you a prepaid return label within 24 hours
  3. Pack the item securely in its original packaging
  4. Attach the return label and drop off at any authorized carrier
  5. You'll receive your refund within 5-7 business days after we receive the item

Yes! We offer free exchanges for different sizes or colors. Contact us at return@lunarisshop.online and we'll arrange the exchange for you. The replacement item will ship as soon as we receive your return.
